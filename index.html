Questions

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Pro comes with a 7-day free trial, and the free plan lets you browse BAP's leads and unlock 10 a month. You can also explore the demo at app.buybap.com/#demo without an account.

How many leads do I get?

BAP's own leads are unlimited on every paid plan: county records in 29+ states, updated daily. The monthly numbers on each plan (150, 400 or 800) are extra on-demand nationwide leads for counties BAP doesn't cover yet.

Where does the lead data come from?

Public county and city records: tax rolls, foreclosure notices, code enforcement and vacant-building lists. On-demand nationwide leads come from a national property-records provider. You can also bring your own lists and properties.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, online in the app under Services → Manage billing. See the refund policy.

Is BAP on iPhone?

Yes. Open app.buybap.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and BAP opens like an app.
