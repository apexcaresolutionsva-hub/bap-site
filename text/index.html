Texting with BAP

BAP (Apex Care Solutions VA LLC dba BAP) only texts homeowners who contact us first or check the texting box on our form. We never buy, rent or share phone numbers, and we never send texts to people who haven't asked to hear from us.

How homeowners opt in

1. Postcard: the homeowner texts us first

BAP mails postcards to property owners offering to buy their house. The back of every postcard shows our number, (469) 224-1122, and invites the owner to call or text YES for a cash offer, with the disclosures shown below. Texting that number is the owner's opt-in. Our first reply confirms it.

2. Website form: optional checkbox

On our cash offer form, homeowners can check an optional, unchecked-by-default box agreeing to receive calls and texts from BAP about their property, with message frequency, rates, STOP/HELP and links to our Privacy Policy and Terms. Checking it is not required to submit the form.

3. Phone: they ask us to text

If a homeowner calls us and asks us to text them, we record when and how they agreed before any text is sent. Our software keeps texting locked on every number until permission is recorded.

What we send

Replies about the homeowner's property: answering their questions, scheduling a call or visit, and following up on the cash offer they asked for. Message frequency varies with the conversation.

First reply (opt-in confirmation): "BAP: Thanks for reaching out about your property. You'll get replies about your offer. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Example: "Hi, this is Dwayne with BAP. Thanks for texting about your property at 123 Main St. Is now a good time for a quick call to go over your cash offer? Reply STOP to opt out."

How to stop or get help

Reply STOP at any time and you will receive one confirmation and no further texts. Reply HELP for help, or contact support@buybap.com or 214-477-7727. Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
